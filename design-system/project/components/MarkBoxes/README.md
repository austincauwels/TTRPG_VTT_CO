Mark boxes are inked squares on the trauma record (Form C.O. 14): Body, Brain and Bleed, three each.

Hand-written from the `.trauma-*` and `.mark-*` rules in `frontend/src/index.css`.

## Anatomy
- `co-mark`: 20px square, 2px `ink` border, `shadow-well`.
- `.filled`: `oxblood`, inked by hand at its own fixed tilt.
- On narrow sheets and touch screens the three tracks stack with 44px boxes.
- The status is rubber-stamped at the end of the line; the fourth mark triggers the scar sequence.
