# Desk papers

The owner's own choice of old cryptid prints, which lie on the chapter hub's desk as aged, used paper (`frontend/public/images/cryptids/`, placed by `CryptidSketches.jsx`). Each is shown whole, never recolored; the aging (torn and deckled edges, stains, foxing, creases) is drawn over it in code by `paperArt.js`.

- `sea-monster.webp`: a sea monster woodcut, as a page torn from a book.
- `bestiary-lions.webp`: a bestiary page of lions and panthers, as a vellum leaf with worn gilt.
- `sea-monk.webp`: a hand-coloured sea monk, pinned with a brass tack.
- `blemmye.webp`: a blemmye woodcut, as a sepia photograph with album corners.
- `sea-swine.webp`: a sea swine woodcut, as a picture postcard.
- `red-ink-creature.webp`: a red-chalk creature study, as a sketchbook leaf (approved 2026-10-05).

No paper is smaller than a railway ticket on the same screen. The official Darrington Press art (the Fairelands map, the investigator role portraits, the three field sketches) is not in this system: it stays in the app, whole and credited, and is never copied into new material.
