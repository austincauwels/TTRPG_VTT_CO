# Personas (second playtest, 11 October 2026)

The same five people as the first playtest, on the same devices, with new investigators, so
that the roles and abilities the first run never used get played. The campaign is new.

## dm: the Lightkeeper (username `lightkeeper_rue`)
Runs the game on a 1440x900 laptop. An experienced game master who ran the first playtest
on this site three days ago, so she knows her way around but not the changes since. Uses
every Lightkeeper tool the site offers when the story calls for it: approving and rejecting
join requests, circle formation and the circle name's Edit, the dispatch (with or without its
template), the tension hourglass and its countdown timer, her own and secret rolls, giving
marks, the trauma record's Edit, the circle page (resources, reports, the Illumination total,
advancement), the notebook and map, pass notes, End Assignment, inviting a player back, a
second campaign and retiring a campaign. Judges the site on: can I run a session without
stopping the table to fight the interface? Do I always know what my players' sheets say?

The campaign is **The Lantern Ward**, two short assignments:

1. **The Lamplighter's Debt.** In the gaslit borough of Hollin Cross, the lamps on Wick Street
   burn green after midnight, and anyone who walks under them wakes owing a debt they cannot
   remember taking on: a stranger's name inked on the inside of their wrist. The circle is sent
   to find the lamplighter. Scenes: the shuttered gasworks; the debtors' ward of St. Brede's
   infirmary; the lamplighter's ledger, which is still being written. This assignment ends in a
   death: one investigator (Bram's) pays the debt in full.
2. **The Glass Orchard.** A short second case, a week later: in a disused hothouse at Kettering
   Park, fruit made of glass has started to grow, and each piece holds a reflection of someone
   who is not in the room. Scenes: the hothouse at dawn; the gardener's cottage.
