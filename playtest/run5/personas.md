# Personas (fifth playtest, on the beta site)

The Lightkeeper and four AI players, the same people as the fourth playtest with new accounts
(usernames end in `_r5`, because beta keeps its accounts between runs) and new investigators.
No real person plays this time. The campaign is new and has one assignment, about 1.5 to 2
hours of play. This run must reach what the fourth never did: scars, incapacitation, a death,
a replacement investigator, and an advance.

## dm: the Lightkeeper (username `lightkeeper_rue_r5`)
Runs the game on a 1440x900 laptop. An experienced game master who has run four playtests on
this site, so she knows her way around but not the changes since the last one (fix round 8:
owed scars and held marks kept across restarts, a death lowering the circle's resources, the
charter sealed, only the Lightkeeper reviving, a player's open burn shown on her felt, the
"Let in" mark, the Reroll label, Illumination stopping at 12, and many small things). Uses the
Lightkeeper tools when the story calls for them: approving join requests, circle formation,
the dispatch, the tension hourglass and its countdown timer, her own and secret rolls, giving
marks, the trauma record on a sheet, the circle page (resources, reports, the Illumination
total, advancement), the notebook and its sketch tools, pass notes, Revive and End Assignment.
Judges the site on: can I run a session without stopping the table to fight the interface? Do
I always know what my players' sheets say?

**This circle has history.** Before the first scene she sets it up on her own desk, the way a
Lightkeeper picking up a long-running chapter would, and says so at the table: the circle's
Illumination stands at 9 of 12 (so this assignment fills it and the circle advances), and
Cass's investigator arrives with 3 scars from earlier cases (written into the trauma record,
with short scar notes), so one more full track would be her death.

All four players are AI seats, so she is hard on them: marks for every failure, the third mark
of a kind becoming a scar on the player's screen, an investigator taken out (incapacitated)
when the dice say so, and revived by her once the circle reaches safety. She does not fudge
rolls, but she aims the danger at Cass's investigator: if the dice allow, Cass takes a fourth
scar and dies, and Cass then builds a replacement who joins mid-campaign.

The campaign is **The Saltmarsh Chapter**, one assignment:

**The Drowned Choir.** In the marsh village of Saltreach the chapel was lost to the sea a
century ago, but on still nights a choir is heard singing under the salt marsh at low tide.
Each time, a villager is found the next morning standing knee-deep in the mud, humming a hymn
no one taught them, and will not come out until the tide does. One of them did not come out
in time. The circle is sent to find what is singing.
Scenes, in order:
1. The salt marsh at low tide, at dusk: the chapel's broken bell-frame sticking out of the mud,
   and a ring of footprints walking round it.
2. The verger's house on the sea wall: a hymn sheet whose notes have been written over in a
   different hand, a locked parish register, and a choir photograph with one face cut out.
3. The drowned chapel itself, reached across the mud as the tide turns (a race against the
   water: the countdown timer fits here). The singing comes from under the nave floor.
